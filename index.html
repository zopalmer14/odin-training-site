 Locations & Attractions

Why Make a Site About This?

I've always wanted to visit Japan and thought that, if I needed to make a site, maybe it'd be fun to make one oriented around that. My supervisor also happens to be going soon as a member of the 30th Class of Mansfield Fellows, which is a cool coincidence.

Why Japan in Particular?

I've been interested in travelling to Japan for some time. I'm curious about all kinds of stuff, but am particularly fascinated by certain aspects of their culture, such as the cuisine and media (e.g., anime, manga, live-action television and movies), and by the variety of geographical features and unique regional quirks and attractions that you can discover by venturing around Japan's prefectures.

Prerequisites to Travelling

There are a number of things I need to do before travelling:

  1. Identify interesting locations and attractions
  2. Learn at least a little of the language
  3. Build an itinerary
  4. Find and book flights
